About YFYD

A Structured Youth Organization With Corporate Hierarchy

YFYD is designed to bridge the gap between academics and the corporate world. Our systems are intentionally built with role clarity, departmental ownership, and measurable execution standards.

Corporate Department Framework

Ten Departments Driving Real-World Exposure

These departments are designed to give members practical corporate experience before entering professional careers.

Marketing Department

Growth planning, campaign execution, and market communication.

Human Resources (HR)

Onboarding systems, talent nurturing, and people operations.

Finance

Budgeting, cost control, and transparent financial administration.

Operations

Planning calendars, process execution, and team coordination.

Legal

Policy awareness, compliance habits, and governance readiness.

Technical

Platform support, digital systems, and technical enablement.

Branding

Visual standards, positioning clarity, and identity consistency.

Communications

Internal coordination and external institutional communication.

Mentorship

Guided growth through experience-sharing and role coaching.

Training

Skill-focused sessions that build execution confidence.

Leadership

Board Members

The board leads strategic direction, departmental integration, and execution quality across the entire YFYD community.
